LogiPuz
Privacy Policy

This policy explains how AppFactoryCloud ("the developer") handles information in the iOS app "LogiPuz – Logic Puzzles" ("the app").

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information or usage data, such as your name, contact details, location, device identifiers or how you use the app. There is no account system.

2. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to the developer or any third party:

  • Records of solved puzzles (date and time, solving time, number of hints and mistakes, the puzzle grid, favorite marks)
  • The grid of a puzzle in progress
  • Settings (mistake display, sound, haptics, appearance, etc.) and how many free puzzles you've played today

This information may be included in your device backups (such as iCloud Backup). Deleting the app deletes it from your device.

3. Ads, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

4. Apple services

  • In-App Purchase: Purchases and restores are processed by Apple's App Store. The developer never receives your payment information.
  • Game Center: Achievements and leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the achievements you earn and your Lv5 average times for leaderboards are sent to Game Center. Game Center data is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer does not collect or store any player information.

5. Sharing you choose to do

Exporting records and sharing results or puzzles happen only when you choose where to share them. What is shared is limited to the puzzle grid, results such as your time, or the records file you choose. The app never sends anything automatically.

6. Children

Because the app collects no information, it collects no information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, email appfactorycloud@gmail.com.
